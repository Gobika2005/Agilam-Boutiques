# MangaiMart — SEO Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Branch:** `fix/seller-console-audit-2026-08`
**Method:** read of the SEO layer + `npm run verify:seo` executed against the **live
Supabase database**, plus direct PostgREST probes of the live catalogue.
Every number below was measured, not estimated.

---

## Executive summary

The SEO *machinery* on this site is genuinely strong — stronger than most
production marketplaces. The edge middleware server-renders titles,
descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph, JSON-LD and a crawlable `<noscript>` body
for every public page before a byte of JavaScript ships; there is a split
sitemap index, a Google Merchant Center feed, LCP preloading, one-hop canonical
host redirects and a 40-assertion regression suite. **All 43 checks pass.**

The problem is not the machinery. **It is that there is almost nothing in it.**

| Measured | Value |
|---|---|
| Live active products | **16** |
| Approved boutiques | **9** |
| Total URLs in sitemap | **64** |
| Merchant Center feed items | **16** |
| Categories with ≥1 live product | **8** |

Of the 11 categories in the audit brief, **3 exist** (Sarees — *1 product*,
Kurtis — 3, Salwar Suits — 1) and **8 do not exist at all**: Dress Materials,
Jewellery, Handbags, Footwear, Accessories, Kids Wear, Men's Wear, Beauty
Products. Category landing pages are generated from live product rows, so those
eight are not weak pages — they are `noindex` soft-404s. No amount of metadata
work changes that; only inventory does.

**Verdict: the technical SEO is ~90% done. The content supply is ~5% done.**

## Critical issues

### C1 — Test reviews will be published to Google as Review markup

**Severity: blocker.** The live `reviews` table contains joke test content
posted under a real name:

> "⚠️ Warning: Don't order anything… unless you're trying to buy imaginary
> clothes. These are dummy products made just for testing."
> "If you're reading this, remember: it's a test product. Don't blame us if
> nothing arrives!"

These are attached to live, active, indexable products carrying 5★
`aggregateRating`. This session added `Review` structured data (§3), which means
**this text now goes into the JSON-LD and the crawlable body of those product
pages.** Verified emitting on `/products/indigo-floral-co-ord-set-9b3a7083`.

Two things are wrong independently of my change: a 5★ aggregate rating built
from a joke review is a review-spam manual-action risk, and the same rows are
already visible to any buyer who opens the reviews panel.

**Fix before deploy:** purge the test reviews (same class of problem as
`purge_seed.sql`, [[seed-data-purge]]). This is the owner's hand — I have not
deleted live rows.

### C2 — Coimbatore is unreachable; "Cbe" is indexable instead

One boutique's `city` is stored as **`Cbe`**. Measured:

```
/boutiques/cbe        → "Boutiques in Cbe — Verified Ethnic Wear Shops"  index, follow
/boutiques/coimbatore → "Page Not Found"                                 noindex, nofollow
```

MangaiMart is headquartered in Coimbatore, and "boutiques in Coimbatore" is one
of the highest-intent local queries the site could win. It is currently a
noindex 404, while an abbreviation nobody searches for is indexed in its place.
The same class of defect will recur every time a seller types a city loosely.

**Fix:** normalise `boutiques.city` on the live rows (`Cbe` → `Coimbatore`), and
constrain city entry at onboarding rather than free text. Needs a migration and
the owner's hand.

### C3 — Eight of eleven target categories have no page

Not a bug — a content gap, but it is the single largest ranking constraint. With
16 products the site cannot rank for "MangaiMart Sarees" (1 saree listed), and
cannot compete at all for jewellery, footwear, kids' or men's wear.

## What was fixed in this session

All changes are committed to the working tree, typecheck clean (`tsc -b` exit 0),
lint clean (0 errors), and `npm run verify:seo` passes with a new assertion added.

### 1. Brand entity — the biggest brand-SEO gap

`middleware.js` served crawlers an Organization node with a name, a URL, a logo
and a description. No contact details, and **no `alternateName`** — so nothing
anywhere on the site told a search engine that *"Mangai Mart"* (two words, an
explicit target query) is the same entity as *MangaiMart*.

Worse, the client-side node in `src/lib/schema.ts` shared the **same `@id`** but
declared `name: COMPANY.legalName` = `"MangaiMart Private Limited"` — a value
still marked `TODO` in `src/data/company.ts`. Two nodes, one identity, two
different names, one of them an unverified corporate claim.

- `middleware.js` — added `alternateName: ["Mangai Mart", "MangaiMart Boutique",
  "MangaiMart India"]`, `email`, `telephone`, `foundingDate` and a
  `contactPoint` with `availableLanguage: ["en", "ta"]`.
- `src/lib/schema.ts` — `name` is now the trading name; `legalName` is **not**
  emitted until the real incorporated entity is confirmed.
- `scripts/verify-seo.mjs` — new `brand entity` check asserting on the node's
  *contents*, not just its presence. It was present and hollow before.

### 2. `og:image` dimensions contradiction

`middleware.js` deliberately removed `og:image:width/height` (1200×630 matches
none of the images actually served — product photos are portrait, the logo is
1254×1254 square) and documented why. `src/lib/pageMeta.ts` re-added them on
every SPA navigation. Removed, with the reasoning carried across so the two
halves stop disagreeing.

### 3. Review structured data — was never emitted anywhere

`productSchema()` in `src/lib/schema.ts` accepted a `reviews` option that **no
caller ever passed**, and the edge emitted none. The reviews live inside a
collapsed accordion (`ProductReviews`), which does not even mount until a buyer
opens it — so no crawler and no AI assistant had ever read a word a customer
wrote about a piece.

Added `productReviewNodes()` in `middleware.js`: the newest 5 non-hidden reviews
as `Review` nodes, plus the same text in the `<noscript>` body (review markup is
only legitimate while the review is visible on the page — the rule `/help`'s FAQ
block already follows). Gated on `reviews_count > 0`, so unreviewed products —
most of the catalogue — cost no extra round trip.

**⚠️ See C1: do not deploy this until the test reviews are purged.**

### 4. Image filenames

Every photo was stored as a bare UUID (`<boutique-id>/9f3c1a2b-….jpg`). Now
`mangaimart-<subject>-<uuid>.jpg`:

- `src/lib/uploadImage.ts` — new `fileStem()` + optional `nameHint`.
- `uploadProductImage()` takes the product title; `uploadBoutiqueImage()` takes
  the shop name; threaded through `ProductForm`, `BoutiqueProfileEdit`,
  `SellerOnboarding`.

The random id is retained after the stem — `upsert: false` and the one-year
immutable cache header both depend on path uniqueness.

**Honest scope:** this is a weak signal, and it only affects **new** uploads.
The 16 existing products keep their UUID filenames. Alt text and surrounding
copy outrank filenames by a wide margin, and both were already correct.

## Findings by area

### 1 · Brand SEO — **80/100** (was 55)

Fixed above. Remaining: `COMPANY.legalName`, address and grievance officer in
`src/data/company.ts` are still `TODO` placeholders. The Coimbatore street
address is published in the client-side Organization schema — if it is not the
real registered office, that is a factual claim to correct or remove.

### 2 · Homepage SEO — **95/100**

Verified: unique title, 158-char-clamped description, canonical, full OG +
Twitter card, `Organization` + `WebSite` + `SearchAction` JSON-LD, LCP hero
preloaded with a `srcset` that byte-matches `imageUrl.ts`, and a `<noscript>`
body linking every category and the 24 newest pieces. No defects found.

### 3 · Boutique SEO — **85/100**

Every approved shop gets an SEO slug, unique title (`<name> — Boutique in
<city>`), `ClothingStore` (LocalBusiness) schema with postal address, opening
hours, `sameAs`, `priceRange`, `hasOfferCatalog`, `aggregateRating` where real,
and a breadcrumb. Strong.

Data gaps weakening it (measured across the 9 approved shops): **1 has no street
address**, **1 has no opening hours**, **4 have no Instagram** — so those four
lose the `sameAs` link that consolidates the shop with its own Instagram
profile, which is exactly what a boutique's own-name search competes against.

`hasMap` / geo coordinates: `boutiques.map_url` exists (migration 0021) but the
edge never reads it. Cheap win — see P2 below.

### 4 · Product SEO — **90/100**

Verified on a live product: `Product` + `Offer` (price, `priceValidUntil`,
availability, condition, seller) + `AggregateRating` + `BreadcrumbList`, keyword
slug, canonical, 301 from bare UUID to canonical slug, gallery images as an
array, crawlable `<noscript>` body with H1, price, description, specs and
onward links. `Review` added this session.

Gap: **1 of 16 products has no description**, so its meta description falls back
to the generated template.

Note: `hasMerchantReturnPolicy` and `shippingDetails` are emitted **client-side
only** (`src/lib/schema.ts`) — the edge `Offer` omits both. Google reads the
edge copy. Low priority, but it is free merchant-listing eligibility.

### 5 · Category SEO — **45/100**

Mechanically correct: `CollectionPage` + `ItemList` + `BreadcrumbList`, unique
title, crawlable body with up to 30 product links. The `/occasions/*` "wear
wear" bug is fixed and regression-tested.

Two real weaknesses:

- **Descriptions are arithmetic, not copy** — *"7 kurta sets pieces from 3
  verified boutiques, from ₹899."* Accurate, but it ranks for nothing and reads
  as generated. These pages are built to win head terms and have no editorial
  text to win them with.
- **8 of 11 target categories do not exist** (C3).

**On the FAQ schema requested for category pages: I recommend not adding it.**
Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health sites in August
2023. On a commerce category page it produces no rich result, and inventing Q&A
to carry markup risks a structured-data manual action. The `/help` FAQPage is
legitimate (real, visible Q&A) and should stay.

### 6 · Image SEO — **75/100**

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Responsive `srcset` | ✅ 5 widths, 240–1600 |
| WebP | ✅ negotiated by Supabase transformer from `Accept` |
| Compression | ✅ q70 thumbs / q82 detail — measured 2116 kB → 64 kB |
| `width`/`height` | ✅ 800×1000 intrinsic, CLS-safe |
| Lazy loading | ✅ `loading="lazy"`, eager + `fetchpriority=high` on LCP |
| Alt text | ✅ descriptive — `"<title> — <category> from <boutique>, <city>"` |
| Filenames | ⚠️ fixed this session, **new uploads only** |
| `<title>` attribute | ❌ not set (see below) |
| Caption | ❌ not set |

The `title` attribute and captions were requested. **I recommend against
both.** `title` on an image produces a hover tooltip, is explicitly not a
ranking signal, and duplicating alt text into it is a known accessibility
anti-pattern — screen readers may announce it twice. Captions matter for
editorial imagery, not catalogue grids. Alt text is the signal that counts and
it is already good.

Sitemap images carry `<image:loc>` and `<image:title>` — correct.

### 7 · Brand search — **80/100**

After the `alternateName` fix, the site can plausibly own: *MangaiMart*, *Mangai
Mart*, *MangaiMart Boutique*, *MangaiMart Fashion*, *MangaiMart Online
Shopping*, *MangaiMart India*.

*MangaiMart Sarees* and *MangaiMart Kurtis* are constrained by inventory (1 and
3 products). *MangaiMart Tamil Nadu* is constrained by C2 — the state's largest
target city has no page.

### 8 · Technical SEO — **92/100**

All verified passing: robots.txt (with all four `Sitemap:` lines — Bing reads
only the first and never expands a `<sitemapindex>`), split sitemap index,
canonical tags, soft-404 `noindex` on unknown products/boutiques/categories/
routes/cities, legacy `/buyer/*` 301s, one-hop `www` + `*.vercel.app` → apex
redirect with no loop, preview deploys walled off with `Disallow: /`, AI
crawlers explicitly allowed, HTTPS + HSTS preload.

Core Web Vitals **architecture** (not measured): edge LCP preload byte-matched
to `imageUrl.ts` and regression-asserted; Supabase preconnect in both CORS
modes; intrinsic dimensions on every image for CLS; async font loading;
route-level code splitting; icon font subset 457 kB → 32 kB.

One observation: `jspdf` (390 kB) and `html2canvas` (201 kB) are in the build.
Confirm they are seller/admin-only chunks and never on a buyer route.

### 9 · Structured data — **90/100**

| Type | Status |
|---|---|
| Organization | ✅ enriched this session |
| WebSite + SearchAction | ✅ |
| Product / Offer / AggregateRating | ✅ |
| BreadcrumbList | ✅ all page types |
| CollectionPage + ItemList | ✅ |
| LocalBusiness (`ClothingStore`) | ✅ |
| FAQPage | ✅ `/help` only — correct |
| Review | ✅ **added this session** (blocked by C1) |

### 10 · Social — **85/100**

OG (site_name, locale `en_IN`, type, title, description, url, image, image:alt)
and `summary_large_image` Twitter cards on every page, server-rendered — so
WhatsApp, Facebook and Slack previews work without JS. `product:price:amount` /
`product:availability` on PDPs, which is what Pinterest Rich Pins read.

Gaps: no `twitter:site` handle — **MangaiMart appears to have no X account**; I
did not invent one. No dedicated 1.91:1 share image, which is why `og:image`
dimensions are correctly omitted (§2). Commissioning one is a real, cheap win.

### 11 · AI search — **90/100**

Genuinely well positioned, and this is underrated. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot,
PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended are explicitly allowed, and every
public page ships real HTML in the first response rather than an empty
`#root` — which is precisely what non-rendering assistants need. The `<noscript>`
prerender is the single highest-value thing already built here.

### 12 · Local SEO — **60/100**

Per-city landing pages exist and are indexable, `ClothingStore` nodes carry real
addresses and hours, `geo.region=IN`. Undermined by C2 and by the missing
address/hours/Instagram rows in §3. `map_url` unused.

Live cities: Dharapuram, Erode, Tirupur, Dindigul, **Cbe**, Pethapampatti.

### 13 · Security SEO — **95/100**

HSTS preload, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`,
`Permissions-Policy`, COOP. Canonical consistency enforced by 301. No duplicate
content found — query strings are stripped from canonicals and `/search` is
disallowed.
